# Path: What experiments actually say

For readers who want the observational half of the story on its own: how a
metric's post-Newtonian content gets summarized in two numbers, which
experiments see which number, and exactly what a headline constraint like
"$\beta-1\lesssim10^{-4}$" does and doesn't assume. This path ends at the honest
fine print, not just the punchline.

1. [`F6` — The Newtonian limit](../part1-foundations/f6-newtonian-limit.md) — how $-g_{tt}\approx1+2\Phi/c^2$ emerges, and why leading order alone can never distinguish anything — the reason this whole path exists.
2. [`WEAK1` — Weak-field degeneracy](../part3-building-the-family/weak1-weak-field-degeneracy.md) — precisely where the family members start to differ: $O(x^2)$, with coefficient $(1+\lambda)/2$ — the target the rest of this path learns to measure.
3. [`PPN1` — The PPN formalism, gently](../part6-ppn-observation/ppn1-ppn-formalism.md) — $\gamma$ (space curvature per unit potential) and $\beta$ (time–time nonlinearity) as the two numbers that summarize post-Newtonian content; GR's $\gamma=\beta=1$.
4. [`PPN2` — Areal vs. isotropic coordinates](../part6-ppn-observation/ppn2-areal-isotropic.md) — the coordinate transformation needed before $A_\lambda$, written in areal coordinates, can be compared to the standard PPN metric at all.
5. [`PPN3` — $\gamma=1$, $\beta=\lambda+2$](../part6-ppn-observation/ppn3-gamma-beta.md) — the payoff: the family's closure coefficient $\lambda$ *is* the measurable nonlinearity $\beta-2$.
6. [`PPN4` — The classical tests, sorted by parameter](../part6-ppn-observation/ppn4-classical-tests.md) — redshift and light bending depend only on $\gamma$ (blind to $\lambda$, since $\gamma=1$ for every member); only perihelion precession depends on $\beta$ too.
7. [`PPN5` — Perihelion precession & the numbers](../part6-ppn-observation/ppn5-perihelion-precession.md) — the precession factor $(2-\lambda)/3$ worked through the family's numbers, from 43″/century at $\lambda=-1$ down to 14″/century at $\lambda=+1$; Mercury rules almost all of them out.
8. [`PPN6` — From $\beta$ to $\lambda$: reading constraints critically](../part6-ppn-observation/ppn6-reading-constraints.md) — how a real headline bound like LLR+Cassini's $\beta-1=(1.2\pm1.1)\times10^{-4}$ or INPOP20a's is actually built, which of its assumptions (like $\eta_N=4\beta-\gamma-3$) go beyond this one-body exterior, and why the honest reading is order-of-magnitude: $|\lambda+1|\lesssim10^{-4}$.
