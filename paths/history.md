# Path: The history

Michell to the modern self-coupling bootstrap, with the popular myths corrected
along the way. This path is short and mostly narrative — it assumes you're
willing to take the family's construction and the PPN/PG results on trust from
elsewhere in the book, and follow instead the two-century arc of how people have
tried (and sometimes failed) to get from "a simple argument" to "the Schwarzschild
metric."

1. [`HIST1` — Michell & Laplace: right number, wrong reason](../part7-michell-pg/hist1-michell-laplace.md) — the 1784 dark-star calculation that lands on $r=2GM/c^2$ by applying Newtonian escape velocity outside its domain of validity; right number, no horizon, no derivation.
2. [`S2` — Deriving Schwarzschild the standard way](../part2-schwarzschild/s2-deriving-schwarzschild.md) — the honest 1916 vacuum-Einstein derivation, so you can see exactly how much more Michell's argument would have needed to actually reach it.
3. [`SYN1` — A century of shortcuts, mapped](../part8-synthesis/syn1-century-of-shortcuts.md) — the full lineage in one table — Lenz–Sommerfeld, Rindler's counterexamples, Gruber et al.'s no-go, Visser, Czerniawski, Kassner, Shuler, Casadio, Deser/Duff — each sorted into *build* or *select*.
4. [`HIST2` — The factor of two in light bending](../part7-michell-pg/hist2-factor-of-two.md) — a second historical correction: why Einstein's 1911 estimate (time part only) gave half the deflection his 1915 full theory predicts.
5. [`OUT1` — Exit ramp: the self-coupling bootstrap](../part8-synthesis/out1-self-coupling-bootstrap.md) — the modern end of the lineage: Deser (1970) and Duff (1973) show that demanding the gravitational field be self-consistent under its own coupling forces Schwarzschild *dynamically* — the "extra information" static accounting never had.
